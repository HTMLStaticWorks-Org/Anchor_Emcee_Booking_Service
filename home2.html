Our Agency Standards Talk to a Producer

In-Ear Monitor (IEM) Fluency

Our hosts take live voice-of-producer cues via in-ear monitors while speaking—stretching or compressing segments gracefully without breaking composure.

Every booking includes bespoke script drafting—from dignitary protocol titles and sponsor tags to custom couple trivia and award citations.
